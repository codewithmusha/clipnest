Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026. This is a starter policy for the ClipNest project. The website owner must review and customise it for their actual operations, legal requirements and service providers before production use.

Video links and temporary files

When you paste a supported link, ClipNest sends it to our server to retrieve video information. If you request a permitted download, the server may store the resulting file temporarily to make it available to you. In the current implementation, completed files are eligible for removal after about 15 minutes; free hosting can remove them sooner during a restart.

Server logs

Hosting providers may process request details, IP addresses and technical logs for security and operation. The application also applies per-IP request limits in temporary memory.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is off unless the website owner configures it. If enabled, ClipNest requests your permission before loading Google Analytics. You can refuse. When enabled, basic visit information and event names such as download started or completed may be collected. The app's event tracking does not intentionally send pasted video links or titles to Google Analytics. Your browser may retain your choice locally.

Third-party platforms

ClipNest is independent of the platforms linked by users. We do not ask you to enter your social media account password or upload authentication cookies.

Contact and corrections

For policy questions, contact the website operator through the contact method they publish. The website owner should add their actual contact details before launch.
